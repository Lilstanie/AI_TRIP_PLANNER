# TP-381: themes and accent colours in Settings

## What changed

- `apps/web/lib/account/theme.ts`, `apps/web/app/styles/themes.css`: seven themes (Default, Island,
  Sunset, Forest, City night, OLED black, High contrast) and five accents, applied through
  `data-scheme`, `data-palette` and `data-accent` on `<html>`.
- `settings.ts` gains optional `theme` and `accent`; `SettingsProvider`, `layout.tsx` and `TripMap`
  use the shared resolver; Settings > Your account has Theme, Accent colour and Appearance rows.
- `tests/e2e/themes.e2e.mjs` and `themes.failures.md`; `settings.e2e.mjs` clicks the renamed
  Appearance row.
- Docs: Themes section in `docs/design/ui-guidelines(.zh).md`, Your account in
  `docs/workspace-ui(.zh).md`, Agent Note `2026-11-09-app-themes.md`.

## Validation

- `themes` E2E: 60/60 against `next dev` with `DATA_MODE=mock`.
- `first-paint` 4/4 and `settings` E2E pass.
- `glass-calibration` and `liquid-glass` fail the same checks on `origin/main` (2a3e20c) as on this
  branch, so those failures predate this change.
- Web typecheck, lint (no new warnings in touched files) and Vitest 688/688.

## Notes for the next person

The map basemap and the native iOS tab bar tint do not follow the theme.
