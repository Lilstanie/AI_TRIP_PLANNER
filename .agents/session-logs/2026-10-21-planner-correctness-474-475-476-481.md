# Correct planner dates, totals and thin-data disclosures

## What changed

- `chat-offline.ts` resolves explicit duration plus start date using inclusive trip-day dates.
- `chat.ts` supplies activity price states, known totals/headroom and reused-place days to reply generation; deterministic fallback aligns its total and caveats.
- Added `planner-correctness.e2e.mjs` and a failure inventory; updated architecture docs in English and Chinese.

## Why

The offline parser previously left duration-only trip dates incomplete. Reply facts lacked activity-level price and reuse context, allowing unsupported free/zero claims or unacknowledged repeated stops. A prior broad scheduling change regressed unrelated orchestrator flows, so this batch discloses reuse without changing scheduling.
