# P3.2 — routing and time

Added explicit WALK/TRANSIT route results, unknown fares, Google route geometry, Time Zone resolution and local wall-time conversion. DST gaps and repeated times are rejected. Transit outside -7/+100 days is explicitly unavailable. No substitute today's timetable and no zero-duration fallback. Each route is queried after the preceding schedule is resolved, with 15 minutes buffer. Transport overlap uses existing orchestrator conflict detection.

Tests passed for Sydney DST boundaries, actual departure date, rounded route duration, 429, unknown fare and route failure. Further live route verification is recorded in P3.4.

The existing injected Maps port now accepts either an RFC 3339 departure instant or a trip date plus local wall-clock time. Google date-based transit calls resolve the origin with Places and Time Zone before calling Routes, reject DST gaps/repeated times, and reject requests outside Google's supported transit window. Itinerary conflict checks pass the previous activity end time; transport uses its actual scheduled 06:00/09:00 departure. Mock and OSM behavior remains compatible.
