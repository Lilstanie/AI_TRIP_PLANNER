# TP-114 Keep the chat scroll position when the top bar switches Conversation and Trajectory

## What changed

- `apps/web/components/chat/ChatPanel.tsx`: the stream records its own scroll position and the panel saves it when the view becomes Trajectory, instead of only inside its private `choose`.
- `apps/web/tests/e2e/trajectory-tab.failures.md`: one new failure line for the top-bar path.
- `docs/e2e-known-failures.md` and `.zh.md`: covered-regression entry for `trajectory-tab`.

## Why

The top bar tabs call `setChatView` directly, so `choose` never ran and the reply that arrived on the Trajectory tab stayed 365 px below the fold. `phone-map-empty-hint` and `sidebar-entries` already pass on the integration branch tip (the #737 hidden-map decision is in the test); nothing to change.

## Validation

- `trajectory-tab --prod`: failed before the fix (gap 365 en and zh), passes after.
- `phone-map-empty-hint --prod`, `sidebar-entries --prod`: pass on the tip without changes.
- web build, typecheck, test (643 passed), verify:docs, pairs, protected, e2e-selectors, e2e-artifacts, format:check-changed: pass; lint: one existing warning in WorkspaceView.tsx.
