# TP-114 Keep the chat scroll position when the top bar switches Conversation and Trajectory

## What changed

- `apps/web/components/chat/ChatPanel.tsx`: the stream records its own scroll position and the panel saves it when the view becomes Trajectory, instead of only inside its private `choose`.
- `apps/web/tests/e2e/phone-map-empty-hint.e2e.mjs`: reads the map panel's visibility from the live DOM.
- `apps/web/tests/e2e/trajectory-tab.failures.md`: one new failure line for the top-bar path.
- `docs/e2e-known-failures.md` and `.zh.md`: covered-regression entry for `trajectory-tab`.

## Why

The top bar tabs call `setChatView` directly, so `choose` never ran and the reply that arrived on the Trajectory tab stayed 365 px below the fold. `sidebar-entries` passes on the tip. `phone-map-empty-hint` already holds the #737 hidden-map decision but read the panel's visibility from a node hydration had replaced (empty string, 1 failure in 6 runs); it now reads the live node.

## Validation

- `trajectory-tab --prod`: failed before the fix (gap 365 en and zh), passes after.
- `phone-map-empty-hint --prod`: one flaky failure before the driver fix, 5 of 5 pass after. `sidebar-entries --prod`: passes on the tip.
- web build, typecheck, test (643 passed), verify:docs, pairs, protected, e2e-selectors, e2e-artifacts, format:check-changed: pass; lint: one existing warning in WorkspaceView.tsx.

`trajectory-tab` also fails on `origin/main` `629cf2a` until this lands; the other two scripts do not apply to `main`.
