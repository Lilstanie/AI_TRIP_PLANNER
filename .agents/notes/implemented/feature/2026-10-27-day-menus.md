# Agent Note: Day menu and itinerary menu

Status: implemented
Owner: spec #743, ticket #750

## Problem

A traveller could not manage a day as a whole or act on the whole itinerary. Lengthening the trip,
emptying a day, dropping a day or parking every stop needed one edit per stop or a replan.

## Decision

- Each day has a menu (Add day before, Add day after, Clear day, Remove day) and a Day map button; the
  itinerary has a menu (Move all events to ideas, Remove all scheduled events). All apply at once as one
  plan revision and record the usual Undo; none opens a dialog.
- The actions are pure plan transforms in `apps/web/lib/trip/day-actions.ts`, applied in the browser like
  the details and note item actions, so they add no edit kind to the server route.
- A day exists because `brief.dates` spans it, so adding or removing a day moves a trip date. Before
  Day 1 the start date moves a day earlier and no booked date changes; anywhere else the end date moves
  a day later or earlier, and stops, stays and flights after the day move with it. A stay that spans the
  new day, or loses a night, is resized and its cost and text are recomputed; `day` on stays, flights
  and their rows is derived from the dates again.
- Refusals show a notice and change nothing: removing a day that holds a flight, removing a night that
  would leave a stay with none, removing the only day (the item is disabled), and clearing an empty day
  (disabled). Invalid results are caught by parsing the plan again.
- Day map selects the day (the map already follows it) and, on a phone or a narrow layout, brings the map
  forward.

## Day title and note

`TripPlan` had no place for a day title or note, and days are only derived from `brief.dates`. Joey
approved a change to `packages/shared` on 2026-10-27 (contract-impact: `packages/shared`):

- `TripPlan.dayNotes` is optional, so every saved plan still parses. It maps a day number, as a string
  key matching `^[1-9]\d*$`, to `{ title?, note? }`; a title is at most 80 characters and a note at most
  500, both trimmed and non-empty. An entry with neither is removed, and an empty map is left out.
- Edit title and Add note (Edit note once a note exists) sit first in the day menu. They open a small form
  under the day heading and save at once as one plan revision with the usual Undo.
- Add day moves the keys of the days it pushes later; Remove day drops the removed day's entry and moves
  later keys one earlier; Clear day keeps the entry because it empties stops, not the day's text.
- Replanning: the browser keeps its `dayNotes` for the days a returned plan still has when that plan
  carries none (`keepDayNotes` in `lib/trip/day-actions.ts`), and a mention revision in the orchestrator
  copies the plan's `dayNotes` into the plan it builds. The notes count as a direct edit, so replacing a
  plan that only has notes asks for the usual confirmation.

## Alternatives considered

- Store title and note as itinerary items of a new kind: they would count as Stops and replan unpredictably.
- Keep them in browser storage beside the plan: they would not follow the plan revision, Undo or replanning.
- Key the notes by date instead of day number: it survives inserting a day, but a trip that moves its
  dates would orphan them, and every other day reference in the plan is a day number.
- A new server edit kind for each action: the transforms touch only the plan, so the extra route and
  preview round trip bought nothing.

## Consequences

- Undo is the existing single-step snapshot, so the next edit replaces it.
- Replanning rebuilds days from the new dates; a manual date change is part of the revision it builds on.
- Day-trip plans (one day, no accommodation) can grow; they cannot shrink below one day.
