## Stage 2. Prototype Implementation (Marks: 20)

In this stage, you will implement a prototype of the architectural design from Stage 1. You will demonstrate your AI-native company to pretend investors (i.e., tutors). Every group should use Jira software to manage the collaboration and practice the Agile software development model.

Your prototype should meet the following criteria:

1. **Implementation of Stage 1 Requirements and Design (7 Marks)**
   Develop the prototype based on the design and requirements from Stage 1. The assessment will be based on the completeness and comprehensiveness of your prototype.
2. **Demonstrating AI Agent Capabilities (4 Marks)**
   Showcase how your AI-native company works, e.g., the agent's ability to perceive, make decisions, and interact effectively within the internal and external systems.
3. **Agile Development Experience (4 Marks)**
   Reflect the group's experience of using the Agile software development model, including iterative development, feedback loops, and team collaboration.
4. **Incorporation of Advanced Technologies (5 Marks)**
   Utilize advanced IT technologies in your prototype to demonstrate innovation. Examples include but are not limited to:
   1. Application frameworks (e.g., React, Flutter)
   2. New devices (e.g., mobile, IoT devices, sensors, AR/VR)
   3. Cloud services (e.g., AWS, Google Cloud)
   4. Deployment systems (e.g., CICD, Kubernetes)
   5. New AI models, tools or techniques (e.g., local/fine-tuned models, AI models with difference capabilities)
   6. Etc. Creativity is encouraged! If you justify the effective use of technology, even one we haven't covered, it will be considered.

**Note:**

Demonstration will be held in the final week. The due date for submitting your source code is **November 1, 2026, at 11:59 PM**. During the Week 13 demonstration, you will be required to download your source code directly from Canvas and deploy it on site. Therefore, please carefully check your code before uploading to ensure it is accurate, error-free, and executable.

This stage is a group-based assessment. Please attach a **contribution statement form** for your group project. If you don't attach it, we assume an **equal contribution** to every member.
