# Partial stay preference failure inventory

Written before the initial stay choice ranked partial matches.

- No stay is rated 8+ with free cancellation, and the choice falls back to the cheapest, so a
  7.2 stay without free cancellation beats a 7.6 stay with it.
- The same happens inside a budget share, which the TP-30 decision meant to prevent.
- A stay rated 8+ with free cancellation stops winning over cheaper partial matches.
- Among equal matches, a dearer stay wins over a cheaper one, raising every estimate.
- A stay whose cancellation terms are unknown is treated as offering free cancellation.
- Mandatory filters (minimum rating, required free cancellation) are bypassed.
